# Privacy Policy – Foundation Studio

*Last updated: 23 November 2026*

Foundation Studio is an academic web application built by Group 1 for the CE 402
Geotechnical Engineering course at IIT Mandi. It can be used without signing in.

**What we collect.** If you choose **Sign in with Google**, we receive your name and email
address from Google. We collect nothing else from your Google account. If you click
**Save Current Project**, the project inputs you entered (soil profile, test data, footing
and loads) are stored with your email so you can open them again.

**How it is used.** Your email is used only to keep your saved projects private to you. We do
not sell, share or use your data for advertising, and nothing is sent to third parties.

**Where it is stored.** Saved projects are stored in Google Cloud Firestore. The app runs on
Google Cloud Run in the United States.

**Your control.** You can delete any saved project with **Delete** in *My projects*. To have all
your data removed, contact the project owner through the support email shown on the Google
sign-in screen or through the project's GitHub repository.

**Cookies.** A sign-in cookie keeps you logged in. No tracking or analytics cookies are used.
